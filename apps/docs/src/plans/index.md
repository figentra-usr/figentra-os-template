# Plans

Plans capture direction that hasn't hardened into an architecture decision or a shipped product yet: roadmaps, phased rollout plans, wave/milestone breakdowns, or a working plan for a piece of work in flight. They're expected to change as reality intervenes — that's the difference between a plan and an ADR (immutable once recorded) or a product blueprint (describes what's already true).

Whoever is driving the initiative writes and maintains its plan page, and should mark clearly when a plan is superseded, completed, or abandoned rather than leaving a stale roadmap looking current.

**Nothing has been written here yet.** Add a plan page when there's a real initiative to track — a migration, a multi-phase build-out, a roadmap for the next few milestones.
