# Company

This section is for information about the company or organization behind the product, rather than the product's architecture or code: brand guidelines, org structure, general "about us" reference material that engineering and other functions both need to point at.

It's written and maintained by whoever owns that material day to day — often ops, brand, or a founder in a small team — and it changes on the company's own schedule, not the engineering release cycle.
