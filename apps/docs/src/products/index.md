# Products

This is where product documentation lives: blueprints, domain overviews, and descriptions of what a given product does for its users. It's the product-facing counterpart to the Platform tab's architecture and engineering material — "what are we building and for whom," not "how is it built."

A product blueprint is typically written by whoever owns that product (product manager, tech lead, or founder in a small team) and updated as the product's scope changes. Unlike an ADR, a blueprint isn't append-only — it should describe the product as it is today, with history left to changelog or ADR pages that reference it.

**Nothing has been written here yet.** This is a blank template with no products defined yet. Add a page (or a sub-folder, for a product with several documents) as soon as there's a first product worth describing.
